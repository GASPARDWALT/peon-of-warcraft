# Visual research references

The art direction was checked against:
- WoW Classic Durotar / Valley of Trials: red earth, sparse dry vegetation, layered red rock.
- Sen'jin Village: coastal sand, palms, tribal huts, tusks, wood, red cloth and water access.
- Echo Isles: tropical foliage, wooden bridges, tusk supports, ruins and coastal water.
- Pokémon Crystal overworld: readable top-down paths, compact environmental silhouettes, limited-color tile construction.

These references inform original assets; no original commercial game tiles are included in this pack.
