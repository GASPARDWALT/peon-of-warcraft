# User-supplied score adaptation

The user supplied six raster pages headed **What's Up**, **4 Non-Blondes**, with the opening marking quarter-note = 64, A-major signature and 4/4 meter. No third-party MIDI, score, lyrics or audio file was downloaded for this conversion.

This is a short eight-bar Game Boy reduction of the refrain, with the four-bar figure repeated. It is not a claimed exact transcription of all seventy measures. The small supplied noteheads limit articulation and octave-double reading; the piano block chords become quiet arpeggios, the bass stays on the wave channel, and a restrained native noise beat supplies the pulse. All four channels have exactly 128 sixteenth ticks and loop together.

`manual_note_stream.csv` and `transcription.json` retain the manually entered notes and simplifications. `whats_up_short_refrain_reference.mid` is a note/rhythm reference, not a recording and not the sound shipped in the ROM. A native APU WAV and timing/priority proof must be captured from the compiled candidate before calling the music playable or tested.
